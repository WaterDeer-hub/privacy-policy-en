Goranism Privacy Policy

Goranism (the "App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is provided in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA).

1. Information Collected and Processed

To calculate your wealth fortune, the App processes the following user-provided information:

  • Name or nickname (Optional)
  • Date of birth (Solar calendar), birth time (Optional), gender
  • Name, birth date, birth time, and gender of profiles added under "Saved People"

The App requires no sign-up and never transmits this information to the developer's server.

To serve advertisements, Google AdMob may directly collect: Advertising ID (Android GAID, iOS IDFA), device and app info, IP address, and ad interaction data.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • To calculate your Four Pillars profile and display wealth scores and insights.
  • To retain your birth details across sessions when "Save Info" is enabled.
  • To deliver daily wealth scores at scheduled times when morning alerts are enabled (scheduled locally on-device without external transmission).
  • To display ads supporting operational costs (Google AdMob).

3. Data Storage and Retention

All entered information is stored exclusively within the App's local storage on your device.

  • Data is deleted immediately when disabling "Save Info" or tapping "Delete Saved Data" in Settings.
  • Removing an entry from "Saved People" permanently deletes that profile immediately.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all remaining stored data from your device.

The retention period for data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Cross-Border Transfers

The App never shares user-entered information with third parties.

For ad delivery, Google collects the ad-related data listed in Section 1 directly, which may be transferred abroad.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (Google AdMob)
  • Destination Countries: Countries where Google operates servers (including the U.S.)
  • Transferred Items: Advertising ID, device/app information, IP address, ad interaction data
  • Timing & Method: Transmitted over network protocols when ads are rendered within the App
  • Purpose & Retention: Ad delivery and metrics ─ subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)

5. User Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may view, edit, or delete your entered information anytime within the App.
  • To opt out of personalized ads, reset or delete your Ad ID in device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads / iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • Users in jurisdictions like the EEA requiring explicit consent can modify ad consent preferences anytime under Settings › "Ad Privacy Settings."

6. Inputting Information of Others

Please obtain proper consent before adding another person's profile to "Saved People." All saved entries remain locally on your device and are never exported elsewhere.

7. Security and Data Protection Measures

Entered data is never transmitted to backend servers and is stored in application-sandboxed local storage, inaccessible to other applications.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us. Because entered information stays on the device, it can also be removed by deleting it in the App or uninstalling the App.

9. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers and devices offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Because there is no common industry standard for DNT, the App does not respond to DNT signals. You can limit ad tracking at any time with your device's advertising settings described in "User Rights and How to Exercise Them."

10. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under California law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

  • We do not sell your personal information for money, and we never receive the birth details you enter — they stay on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier. Under California law, this may be considered "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • To opt out, delete or reset your advertising ID (Android: Settings › Google › Ads) or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • You may send any privacy request to the email address in the contact section below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
